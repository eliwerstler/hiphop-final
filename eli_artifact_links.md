## NOTES ON IMAGE SOURCES

- **Source 8 (Run-DMC / fashion visual):** Getty Images is the best archive for period-accurate photos but images require licensing. The MR PORTER and Business of Fashion articles contain high-quality editorial images that are embeddable with attribution. For the site, consider screenshotting or sourcing public domain / Creative Commons alternatives.
- **Source 7 (Newsweek cover):** The actual magazine cover image can be found via image search for "Newsweek Rap Rage 1990 Tone Loc." Several eBay listings and archival sites have scans of the cover.
